The three tools

Recent checks

    The ASes and paths that you checked lately, with what is different now. They are kept in this browser only (its local storage): nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody else sees them.

    Shows the AS's own ASPA and every ASPA that names it as a provider. Try AS680, AS12779, AS1299 or AS6939.

    Applies the ASPA verification procedure of the IETF draft to the ASPA objects listed on this page. Try 3356 680 or read how it works (opens in a new tab).

    Check many paths at once

    One path on each line, as your router prints them (neighbour first, origin last); a prefix in front of the path is kept in the table. Empty lines and lines that start with # are skipped; up to 5,000 lines.

    Fetches the routes that the RIPE RIS route collectors saw at the last reading of RIPEstat (a snapshot, whose age is shown), checks each prefix and origin against the ROAs, and applies the ASPA verification to every distinct AS path. For an AS, the routes it originates; for an address, the routes of the most specific prefix that covers it. Try 193.0.0.0/21 or AS3333.
